# 尸城守夜

白天出门搜刮：车子、民房、商店、警局、诊所都能进去翻，越远的区域越危险，东西也越好。天黑前得赶回营地，封好缺口，和同伴一起守到天亮；回不来，就在外面找间屋子把门钉死熬一夜，营地那边只能靠留守的人。只有一条命。

**在线试玩：** https://snowwarri0r.github.io/night-unending/

电脑用键盘和鼠标玩；手机横屏用双摇杆玩，左手移动，右手拖动瞄准、松手开火。

这个仓库只放打包好的网页，是个还在开发中的早期版本。音效全部是 CC0 素材，出处见 [audio/AUDIO_SOURCES.md](audio/AUDIO_SOURCES.md)。

*Nightwatch: Zombie City*: scavenge the town by day (every house, shop and police station can be walked into, and the further out, the worse it gets and the better the finds), get back to the camp before dark and hold it with your crew until dawn, or spend the night barricaded somewhere out there while the camp holds on without you. One life. An early build of a browser game, playable at the link above with mouse and keyboard, or on a phone held sideways with twin touch sticks.
